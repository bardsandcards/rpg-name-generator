The response names three legionaries. Each name follows the Roman citizen pattern: a praenomen (such as Gaius, Lucius, Marcus, Quintus, Titus, Sextus), a family nomen (most end in -ius; some attested ones end in -enus, -ienus or -aeus), and usually a cognomen. The three should not all share one praenomen, and none may be "Gaius Valerius Crispus", "Gaius Valerius Maximus" or "Marcus Varro".
